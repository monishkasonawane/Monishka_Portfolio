## 🎨 New Creative Studio UI Design

- **Floating macOS-Style Dock (`.floating-dock`)**:
  - Pinned at the bottom-center with smooth blur, active section indicator, and 1-click teleportation to any section (`Home`, `About`, `Skills`, `GovSolve`, `Projects`, `Resume`, `Events`, `Roadmap`, `Contact`).
- **Immersive Split-Screen Landing Experience (`#hero`)**:
  - High-impact editorial headline, dynamic role ticker, and live status badge.
  - Artistic Studio Portrait Frame showcasing her real photograph with ambient lighting, merit badges, and direct LinkedIn & GitHub cards.
- **Interactive On-Screen Resume Sheet (`#resume`)**:
  - Luxury CV document presentation with professional summary, academic records (**91.35% Diploma Merit** & **RCPIT DSE B.Tech**), technical skill matrix, featured software projects, and experience blocks.
  - Built-in **Print / Save as PDF** (`window.print()` trigger) formatted cleanly for printing/export.
  - 1-click **Copy Resume Text** button.
- **Flagship Project: GovSolve Citizen (`#govsolve`)**:
  - Full civic tech architecture showcase with GPS geotagging, automated civic triage, 4-step real-time pipeline, and administrative telemetry.
- **Campus Events & Hackathons Photo Gallery (`#events`)**:
  - Filterable by `All Events`, `Hackathons & Coding`, `Workshops & Labs`, and `Symposiums & Exhibitions` with detail modal lightbox.
- **Career Roadmap Timeline (`#roadmap`)**:
  - Futuristic 4-phase trajectory from 2026 foundation to long-term tech leadership.
- **Contact & Communication (`#contact`)**:
  - Interactive direct messaging form, 1-click copy email button, and verified contact info (`monishkasonawane@gmail.com`, Shirpur, RCPIT).
